1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Gully Kanche: Marbles Hop ("the Game", "App"), developed and published by Chronicle Play ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our players.

Gully Kanche is primarily an offline casual game. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identities, and do not track your location.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Gully Kanche on your Android device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide an authentic, safe, and frictionless vintage gaming experience, we do NOT collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Your precise or approximate GPS location.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or storage files.
  • Financial details, payment cards, or bank credentials.
  • Biometric data or personal identification documents.

3. Information Collected Automatically & Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates select trusted third-party services to deliver rewarded ads, perform analytics, and maintain stability.

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Our game displays rewarded advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads and prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob may process:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): In accordance with Android 13+ (API 33+) requirements, our app requests the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. This allows Google AdMob to deliver personalized or contextual ads and fight fraud. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in your Android device's Settings under Google > Ads.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, carrier, network connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular), and language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Impressions, views, and rewarded video completion events.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please refer to Google's Advertising Privacy Policies.

B. Gameplay State & Local Storage

All your earned coins, unlocked marble skins (e.g., Cat's Eye, Tiger Spark, Solar Glow), high scores, sound toggles, and completed challenges are saved locally on your device using secure Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to uninstall the application or clear your app data.

4. Device Permissions Used

Our app requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core operation and policy compliance:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load and stream rewarded ad videos and check network availability.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active internet connectivity before requesting advertisements.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during marble flicking, strikes, and board rebounds (can be toggled off inside game settings).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Declared in compliance with Google Play requirements for advertising identification on Android 13+.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Gully Kanche is designed as a family-friendly casual board and arcade game suitable for all general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Ad networks configured in our application adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines and child-directed ad restrictions where applicable.

6. Data Security & Retention

Because we do not operate remote user login servers or cloud user accounts, we do not retain your personal data on external servers. All gameplay progress is contained entirely on your hardware. Ad network communications are encrypted in transit via standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

7. Your Choices & Data Controls

  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: You can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your AAID directly within your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID).
  • Haptic / Sound Toggles: You can mute sounds or disable vibration effects anytime directly from the in-game settings menu.
  • Data Erasure: To clear all local high scores and saved coins, you can tap "Clear Storage / Data" in your device's App Settings for Gully Kanche or uninstall the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates, new legal requirements, or Google Play policy modifications. Any updates will be published with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
